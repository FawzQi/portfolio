# Design System — Industrial Telemetry

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## World & Atmosphere
High-precision instrumentation and avionics telemetry. Clean, grounded, mathematical, and competition-tested. Conveys serious systems engineering, microsecond-critical firmware, and physical hardware control.

## Anti-References (What We Kill)
- "Hi, I'm [Name]" with typewriter title cycling
- Floating pastel blur blobs and rotating dashed borders
- Generic "yellow pill with dot" SectionTitle badges
- Emoji and star badges (`💼`, `🎓`, `🏆`, `★`)
- Generic rounded-3xl SaaS cards with rainbow gradients
- "Always learning — currently exploring..." boilerplate

## Typography
- **Display & Headings:** `Space Grotesk`, sans-serif (deliberate geometric engineering feel)
- **Body:** `Inter` / system-ui, neutral readability
- **Telemetry & Metrics:** `JetBrains Mono`, monospace (pinouts, baud rates, timing, hardware specs)

## Palette (Telemetry Instrument)
- **Canvas:** Dark mode first (`#0B0F17` slate-950), crisp high-contrast light mode (`#F8FAFC` slate-50)
- **Surface:** `#111827` (slate-900), borders `#1F2937` (slate-800) with 1px razor precision
- **Signal Amber:** `#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24` (calibration, active alert, hardware highlights)
- **Telemetry Cyan/Cobalt:** `#0284C7` / `#38BDF8` (data streams, firmware, logic)
- **Status Phosphor Green:** `#10B981` (active systems, compiled status)

## Layout & Components
- Micro-grid lines (1px crisp borders, crosshair corners `+`, technical spec labels)
- Real hardware callouts: Target MCU, Clock, Protocol (CAN/UART/SPI), Real-time jitter
- Compact, dense, high-signal information density for engineering interviewers
