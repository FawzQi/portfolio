# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Engineering hiring managers, technical recruiters, lab directors, and robotics team leads evaluating embedded systems, robotics control, and IoT engineers for roles and research collaborations.

## Product Purpose

Professional engineering portfolio for Ahmad Faiq Fawwaz to demonstrate technical depth, competition-proven robotics achievements, firmware mastery, and end-to-end hardware-software integration.

## Positioning

Hardware meets intelligence: real-time motion planning and national-competition-winning robotics (VI-ROSE ITS) combined with edge AI on constrained microcontrollers (ESP32, STM32, Raspberry Pi).

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile browsers used by interviewers reviewing candidates during technical screening, resume evaluation, and portfolio walk-throughs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 18 + Vite SPA deployed via GitHub Pages (`base: '/portfolio/'`).
- Tailwind CSS with Framer Motion animations and responsive dark/light mode.
- Interactive showcase: Hero, About, Skills, Experience, Projects gallery, Contact form via EmailJS.

## Brand Commitments

- Owner: Ahmad Faiq Fawwaz (fawzqi).
- Tone: Serious technical rigor, competition-tested credibility, crisp modern engineering aesthetic.
- Visual world: Hardware/robotics-inspired, clean contrast, restrained motion.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified profile data: `src/data/profile.json`
- Project portfolio records: `src/data/projects.json`
- Skills breakdown: `src/data/skills.json`
- Work & leadership history: `src/data/experience.json`
- Real competition image assets and CV links on file.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Claims:** Lead with competition awards, measurable hardware metrics, and live repo links.
2. **Restraint & Polish:** Engineering precision over flashy decoration.
3. **Scannable Hierarchy:** Recruiters scan in under 30 seconds; key roles, stacks, and impact must pop instantly.
